Making Curiosity Social

What if every question was an invitation to discover a new world? The Curiosity Quotient (TCQ) is your gateway to all those worlds under one roof, bringing together knowledge, culture and people to create experiences that make learning feel less like learning and more like discovery.

At its heart, TCQ is a culture space. A place for ideas to meet people, for communities to find each other, and for healthy cultures to grow, evolve and discover their place in the microcosm called Chennai.

What is The Curiosity Quotient?

TCQ began in 2023 with a very practical ask: that learning shouldn't have to end with a degree, that curiosity shouldn't have to be a solitary pursuit, and that curious people deserve a place to come home to. There wasn't anything around, so we started building one.

What began with a quiz slowly became a gathering of people who liked knowing things simply because they were interesting. Over time, that grew into a community of more than 2,000 curious people in Chennai.

Since then, TCQ has found its way into classrooms, auditoriums, cafés, galleries, streets and screens. Five wings, founded 2023, 2,000+ curious cats.

Who's behind TCQ: Dr. Vishnu Aravind, MBBS, MD

Born in Chennai, grew up with the internet, and somehow ended up studying medicine, with a habit of asking questions. TCQ connects people to ideas they might not have discovered otherwise, helps schools and colleges open up new worlds for their students, and works with startups, brands and organisations to find interesting ways to connect with their audiences.

What we do: five wings, one curiosity

Quizzes

This is where TCQ was born. The Typical Chennai Question is what gave us our name. Events include 6 to 60 Quizzes at KIS Cafe, Abuzz 2025 (PSBB Group of Schools), Fundamavan '26 (SSN Instincts), Typical Chennai Quiz 2025 for KYN, and The Hungry Quiz for Kraft Collective.

Circles

The crossroads of Chennai's communities: Circles Season One (indie music, stargazing, Madras history, cricket) and Hysterically Speaking / Vinyl Destination with Berty Ashley.

Collabs

Discovery-led experiences for brands and culture: Walls on Wheels for Five Feet Collective, How to Name It? for KYN, and Madras D-Coded, a 40-day puzzle campaign on Madras and its journey to Chennai.

Writes

Millennial long-form content about Chennai, its newest cultures, icons and trends, published on our Substack.

Teaches

Schools beyond exams, colleges beyond degrees: the Intellect Club at MOP Vaishnav College for Women, workshops at Ethiraj College for Women, and a beginner's quizzing workshop at Goethe-Institut Chennai.

What could we make together?

  • Brands and companies: integrated physical and digital campaigns, focus groups and community conversations.
